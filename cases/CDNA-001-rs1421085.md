# CDNA-001 — rs1421085

## Research question

**What mechanism connects the obesity-associated rs1421085 allele to increased `IRX3/Irx3` expression in the posterior hypothalamus?**

This case deliberately separates a well-supported adipocyte mechanism from a partially resolved CNS mechanism.

## Variant

- Variant: `rs1421085`
- Alleles: T>C
- Region: non-coding FTO locus
- Human phenotype association: obesity / BMI-associated locus

## Known branch A — adipocyte progenitors

### A1 — rs1421085 C disrupts an ARID5B repressor motif

```text
rs1421085 T>C
  -> ARID5B repressor motif disrupted
```

**Status:** `rescued`

**Evidence:** Claussnitzer et al. reported that the T>C change disrupts a conserved ARID5B motif. Endogenous CRISPR-Cas9 editing in primary adipocyte progenitors was used in both directions: engineering the risk allele increased `IRX3/IRX5`, while C>T repair restored repression.

**Source:** PMID 26287746 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26287746/

### A2 — loss of repression increases IRX3 and IRX5

```text
ARID5B repression down
  -> IRX3 / IRX5 up
```

**Status:** `rescued`

**Context:** human adipocyte progenitor / early adipocyte differentiation.

**Evidence:** motif perturbation, ARID5B knockdown, allele editing, expression response.

**Source:** PMID 26287746.

### A3 — IRX3/IRX5 elevation shifts adipocyte program and suppresses thermogenesis

```text
IRX3 / IRX5 up
  -> beige/thermogenic program down
  -> mitochondrial thermogenesis down
  -> lipid storage up
```

**Status:** `rescued`

**Evidence:** knockdown in risk-allele adipocytes restored thermogenesis; overexpression in non-risk cells produced the opposite effect; allele repair restored thermogenic programs and oxygen consumption.

**Source:** PMID 26287746.

### A4 — in-vivo locus evidence

Mouse work later linked the rs1421085 cis-regulatory module to tissue-specific `Irx3/Irx5` regulation and organismal metabolic phenotypes.

**Status:** `supported`

**Source:** PMID 34290091 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34290091/

## Branch B — CNS / posterior hypothalamus

A 2025 mouse study created mice harboring the obesity-associated rs1421085 allele and reported:

1. increased susceptibility to diet-induced obesity;
2. increased food intake and decreased energy expenditure under the tested obesogenic conditions;
3. increased brain `Irx3`, including prominent expression/elevation in metabolically relevant regions such as posterior hypothalamus (PH) and dorsal vagal complex (DVC);
4. increasing IRX3 in PH, but not DVC, increased body weight and food intake;
5. increased IRX3 in IRX3-positive PH neurons decreased neuronal excitability/activity;
6. inhibiting IRX3-positive PH neurons increased body weight and food intake.

**Primary source:** PMID 40835181 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40835181/

### B1 — variant to PH Irx3 expression

```text
rs1421085 obesity-associated allele
  -> ???
  -> Irx3 up in posterior hypothalamus
```

**Status:** `candidate gap`

The boundary observations are supported, but the intervening regulatory mechanism is not yet established by the adipocyte evidence alone.

### B2 — PH IRX3 elevation to reduced neuronal excitability/activity

```text
IRX3 up in IRX3+ PH neurons
  -> neuronal excitability/activity down
```

**Status:** `perturbed`

**Source:** PMID 40835181.

### B3 — reduced PH neuronal activity to feeding/body-weight phenotype

```text
IRX3+ PH neuron activity down
  -> food intake up
  -> body weight up
```

**Status:** `perturbed`

**Source:** PMID 40835181.

## Competing hypotheses for GAP-001

### H1 — ARID5B reuse

The same rs1421085 -> ARID5B mechanism demonstrated in adipocyte progenitors operates in one or more PH cell types.

**Would support:**
- the rs1421085 element is accessible in relevant PH cells;
- `ARID5B` is expressed in those cells;
- allele-dependent ARID5B occupancy or enhancer activity exists;
- perturbing ARID5B changes `IRX3` in the predicted direction.

**Would weaken/falsify:**
- no ARID5B expression/occupancy in the relevant cells;
- rs1421085 locus inaccessible in the relevant cell state;
- ARID5B perturbation fails to affect `IRX3` while variant-dependent regulation persists.

### H2 — different transcription-factor mechanism

A different transcription factor recognizes the rs1421085-containing regulatory sequence in PH cells.

**Test:** cell-type-specific motif/footprinting plus allele-specific perturbation.

### H3 — chromatin accessibility mechanism

The allele changes enhancer accessibility/activity without requiring ARID5B as the principal mediator.

**Test:** allele-aware ATAC-seq / chromatin-state evidence and reporter or endogenous perturbation.

### H4 — 3D chromatin-contact mechanism

The allele changes contact probability between the rs1421085 regulatory element and the `IRX3` promoter or another regulatory hub.

**Test:** relevant cell-type chromatin-contact data followed by perturbation of the element/contact anchor.

### H5 — indirect mediator

The variant first changes another gene/pathway, which then alters `IRX3`.

```text
rs1421085 -> mediator X -> IRX3 up
```

**Test:** identify genotype-dependent mediators with correct temporal ordering; perturb mediator X and test rescue.

### H6 — cell-composition effect

Bulk PH `Irx3` elevation reflects a changed abundance/state of IRX3-positive cells rather than higher expression per cell.

**Test:** genotype-aware single-cell or single-nucleus expression analysis.

### H7 — interaction-dependent effect

The CNS effect depends on sex, diet, developmental stage, temperature, hormonal state, or another interaction.

**Test:** stratified experiments/data and explicit interaction models.

## Computational work package v0.1

Before proposing new wet-lab experiments, test what can be resolved with open data.

### WP1 — locus identity and coordinates

- normalize rs1421085 on a declared genome assembly;
- record reference/alternate alleles and surrounding sequence;
- map known linked variants separately from the candidate causal variant.

### WP2 — PH cell-type expression

Search mouse and human hypothalamic single-cell/single-nucleus atlases for:

- `IRX3/Irx3`;
- `ARID5B/Arid5b`;
- candidate transcription factors predicted at the locus.

Goal: identify candidate cell populations where H1-H7 are biologically possible.

### WP3 — regulatory state

For candidate PH cell types, collect available:

- ATAC-seq / DNase accessibility;
- enhancer/promoter chromatin marks;
- TF motif/footprint evidence;
- allele-specific regulatory evidence where available.

### WP4 — target-gene connection

Test evidence that the rs1421085-containing element can regulate `IRX3` in CNS context:

- chromatin contact;
- enhancer-gene maps;
- eQTL/caQTL evidence;
- perturbation datasets.

### WP5 — hypothesis elimination

For each H1-H7, produce one of:

- `SUPPORTED`
- `NOT_SUPPORTED`
- `CONTRADICTED`
- `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`

No hypothesis is promoted merely because it is plausible.

## Current conclusion

The adipocyte branch provides a **positive control** for the CAUSAL-DNA protocol because it contains direct perturbation and rescue evidence.

The CNS branch provides the first **research gap**:

> What is the cell-type- and context-specific regulatory mechanism by which the rs1421085 obesity-associated allele elevates `Irx3` in the posterior hypothalamus?

That question is the first target of CAUSAL-DNA.
